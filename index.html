TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS

Certified technical specifications for Agrosteed heavy tractor backhoe attachments.

Engineering Parameter Standard Specification (Variant : Stredo) Operational Advantage
Tractor Horsepower Compatibility 45 HP to 90 HP (CAT-II / CAT-III Hitch) Fits Mahindra, Swaraj, John Deere, Massey Ferguson, Sonalika, New Holland
Maximum Digging Depth 2,850 mm (9.35 Feet) Ideal for deep irrigation trenches, pipeline laying, and foundation work
Maximum Reach from Swing Pivot 3,850 mm (12.63 Feet) Clears high canal embankments without repositioning tractor
Maximum Discharge / Loading Height 2,420 mm (7.94 Feet) Direct loading into standard agricultural tipper trolleys
Hydraulic Working Pressure 210 Bar / 3,045 PSI High breakout crowd force in rocky, compacted, and black cotton soils
Swing / Slew Rotation Arc 180° Dual-Cylinder Actuation Smooth side dumping without tractor repositioning
Outrigger Stabilizer Spread 2,200 mm Wide Stance Footprint Dual independent hydraulic downforce locking feet for uneven gradients
Bucket Material & Width Hardox 450 with Replaceable Forged Teeth (450 mm width) Resists extreme abrasive wear in stony and gravel-rich quarry soil
Hitch Mounting Time Under 15 Minutes Quick-Lock Tractor remains multi-purpose for ploughing, rotavating, and transport
